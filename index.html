Mathematician → AI researcher, London

I'm a mathematician turned AI researcher based in London. I lead research at TurinTech working on agentic AI and code optimization (search optimization methods, self-improving agents, memory systems).

Before that, I did a PhD in mathematics through the UT Austin–Portugal program (Instituto Superior Técnico & UT Austin), working on mean-field games and optimal control, followed by postdoctoral research at KAUST and a researcher position at the University of Coimbra.
