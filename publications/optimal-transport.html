Introduction to Optimal Transportation

University of Coimbra · 2019

slides optimal-transport

From moving sand to the geometry of probability measures. Starting from Monge's problem of 1781, the slides follow three questions: existence (is there an optimal way to move the mass, via Kantorovich's relaxation), structure (what the optimal map looks like, via duality and convex analysis), and geometry (the optimal cost as a distance on the space of measures, with its geodesics, convexity and gradient flows). Along the way, the isoperimetric and Brunn–Minkowski inequalities are proved by transport.
